## Statistics

- run-up vs reaction correlation: Pearson r = 0.291 (p = 0.334), Spearman ρ = 0.209 (p = 0.494) — n = 13, directional evidence only
- release day closed red in 69% of quarters
- given a 5-day run-up > +5% (n = 3): red close 33% of the time
- 2026-Q2 run-up sits at the 85% percentile of the sample; its reaction at the 8% percentile

## Reading

2026-Q2 (2026-07-02) came in with a +13.3% run-up and a -7.5% reaction (beat on the numbers).
- That reaction is the **most negative release-day close in the sample** — worse than all 12 prior quarters.
- Delivery days lean red at the baseline: 69% of release days closed below the prior close regardless of the numbers.
- Prior quarters with a comparable >+5% run-up: 2023-Q2 (run-up +16.1% → day +1.0%, +5d -3.7%), 2024-Q2 (run-up +14.9% → day +10.2%, +5d +13.8%). 2 of 2 still closed green on the day, so a big run-up alone did not historically guarantee a fade — though where the day held green, the following week often gave it back (see +5d column).
- Conclusion: the 2026-Q2 fade is consistent with sell-the-news positioning, but its severity is unusual — the unwind was sharper than any delivery-day reaction in the sample rather than a routine repeat of past patterns.

*Sample size n = 13 quarters — treat correlations as indicative, not statistically significant. Not investment advice.*
